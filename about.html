Origin

Why Grov exists.

We started as a social media agency. We watched talented service business owners create brilliant content — and still not grow consistently. Not because their content was wrong. Because everything else was disconnected.

Content attracts. But broken messaging, no funnel, and zero follow-up means warm leads go cold every day. We saw it over and over. So we built the answer.

Today Grov Media is a global client acquisition systems agency — strategy, psychology, copy, GHL builds, AI automation, and monthly management. We build the engine. You run the business.
