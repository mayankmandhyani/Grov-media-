A diagnostic approach to identifying the structural flaws in your current acquisition model. The Revenue Audit identifies which of these is costing the most — and what to fix first.

Tap each stage to read more.

01 Traffic Quality
chevron_right

Are you buying clicks or commanding attention from qualified prospects? This is the first place spend gets wasted — before a lead ever reaches your funnel.

02 Capture Rate
chevron_right

The percentage of attention that actually enters your ecosystem. Traffic without capture is spend with no return.

03 Nurture Velocity
chevron_right

The speed and efficiency at which a cold lead becomes a booked call. Every hour a lead waits drops conversion probability sharply.

04 Show Rate
chevron_right

A booked call means nothing if they don't appear. We measure intent, not just bookings — the two are not the same thing.

05 Close Rate
chevron_right

The ultimate metric of alignment between marketing promise and sales execution. Low close rates usually mean leads arrived unprepared.

06 LTV
chevron_right

Lifetime value — the true measure of a sustainable business, and the most expensive leak of all when it's ignored while chasing new leads.
