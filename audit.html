Diagnostic

Where money leaks. Before we fix it.

A diagnostic approach to identifying the structural flaws in your current acquisition model. The Revenue Audit identifies which of these is costing the most — and what to fix first.

01
Lead Response Time

Every hour a lead waits drops conversion probability by 10x. If follow-up is manual, leads are leaving before you've replied.

02
Lead-to-Call Rate

How many interested leads actually book a call? If this number is below 30%, there's a structural breakdown between interest and action.

03
Call-to-Client Rate

Are calls converting or stalling? Low close rates usually mean leads arrive unprepared — the pre-call system is broken, not the pitch.

04
Content Dependency Score

What percentage of your revenue requires you to actively post? If posting stops, does revenue stop? That's a dependency — not a business.

05
Pipeline Velocity

How long does it take a lead to move from first touch to paying client? Slow pipelines lose momentum, and momentum is where conversion lives.

06
Retention & Expansion Revenue

How much revenue comes from existing clients vs constant new acquisition? Ignoring retention while chasing leads is the most expensive leak of all.
