The Methodology

The full path. End to end.

Most agencies own one stage. Grov Media owns the entire path — from first attention to long-term retention.

01

Acquisition

Goal — more qualified leads entering the pipeline

We build the infrastructure that attracts the right people — funnel strategy, lead magnets, quiz funnels, and content systems. Not random traffic. Qualified attention from the right audience.

Funnel strategy · Lead magnets · Quiz funnels · Content architecture

Average improvement: 3x more qualified leads.

02

Conversion

Goal — more of the right leads booking calls

We improve the booking flow, landing pages, and nurture sequences so interested leads convert — instead of going quiet after their first interaction.

Landing pages · Booking flow · Nurture sequences · CTA optimisation

Average lead-to-call rate after system build: 54%.

03

Lead Handling

Goal — zero leads falling through the cracks

AI conversation systems and GHL automations that handle qualification, warm-up, and follow-up without the founder being the bottleneck.

AI conversations · GHL automations · DM systems · CRM pipeline

Time returned per week: ~15hrs from manual lead handling.

04

Sales Conversion

Goal — more calls closing into paying clients

Pre-call nurture, trust-building sequences, and follow-up systems so prospects arrive on calls informed, pre-qualified, and ready — not cold.

Pre-call nurture · Sales copy · Objection handling · Post-call follow-up

Average call-to-client rate after optimisation: 61%.

05

Retention

Goal — stable, recurring revenue from existing clients

Onboarding systems, retention sequences, referral programs, and upsell flows — so growth doesn't require constantly acquiring new clients from scratch.

Onboarding flows · Referral systems · Retention sequences · LTV optimisation

Average LTV improvement within 90 days: 2x.
